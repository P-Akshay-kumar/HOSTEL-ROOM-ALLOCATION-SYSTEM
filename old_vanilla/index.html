    Rules & Regulations

    Important guidelines every resident must follow

    1. Students must carry their ID cards at all times within the hostel premises.
    2. Entry and exit timings: 6:00 AM - 10:00 PM. Late entry requires a gate pass.
    3. Consumption of alcohol, tobacco, and drugs is strictly prohibited.
    4. Ragging in any form is a punishable offence under law.
    5. Students must maintain cleanliness in rooms and common areas.
    6. Electrical appliances like heaters, irons, and cookers are not allowed in rooms.
    7. Visitors must register at the reception and leave by 6:00 PM.
    8. Noise levels must be kept low, especially during study hours (7 PM - 10 PM).
    9. Any damage to hostel property will be charged to the responsible student.
    10. Students must vacate the hostel during official vacation periods unless approved.
    11. Complaints must be reported through the official complaint system.
    12. Room changes are only permitted through the official request system with admin approval.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Find answers to common queries

    How do I apply for hostel accommodation?
    Register on the system, log in, and submit a hostel application from your dashboard. Select your preferred block and room type.
    How is room allocation done?
    Room allocation is done by the hostel administrator based on availability, year of study, and preferred block. The system enforces year-based floor restrictions.
    Can I change my room after allocation?
    Yes, you can submit a room change request. If the target room is empty, it will be auto-approved. Partially filled rooms are also available for selection.
    What are the block/floor restrictions?
    Block A: 1st Year → Floors 1–4, 2nd Year → Floors 4–8, 3rd & 4th Year → Floors 8–12. Block B: 2nd Year → Floors 1–8, 3rd & 4th Year → Floors 8–12. 1st years cannot access Block B.
    How do I pay hostel fees?
    After room allocation, go to the Payment tab in your dashboard. Complete the payment to confirm your room.
    How do I request a gate pass?
    Navigate to the Gate Pass tab in your dashboard and fill the request form. Gate passes and vacation passes are available.
    Can my parents/guardians stay in the hostel?
    Guest accommodation can be requested through the Guest tab. Approval is at the warden's discretion.
    How do I file a complaint?
    Use the Complaint page accessible from the navigation menu. Select a category, describe the issue, and submit.
